# Structra Platform - Client Documentation

## 🏗️ **What is Structra?**

Structra is a comprehensive online platform that connects homeowners with qualified contractors for construction and renovation projects. Think of it as a "LinkedIn for construction" where homeowners can post their projects and contractors can bid on them.

---

## 🎯 **Who Can Use Structra?**

### **Homeowners**
- People who need construction work done on their property
- Want to find reliable, verified contractors
- Need to manage their projects from start to finish

### **Contractors**
- Licensed construction professionals
- Want to find new projects and clients
- Need to build their reputation and portfolio

### **Administrators**
- Platform managers who oversee the entire system
- Handle user verification and platform maintenance

## 🔄 **How It Works**

### **Step 1: Registration**
- Choose your role (Homeowner or Contractor)
- Create your account with basic information
- Verify your email address
- Complete your profile setup

### **Step 2: Profile Setup**

#### **Homeowners:**
- Add your contact information
- Specify your location
- Set your preferences

#### **Contractors:**
- Upload business license and insurance
- Add portfolio photos
- Specify your specialties
- Set your service areas

### **Step 3: Project Lifecycle**

#### **For Homeowners:**
1. **Create Project** - Describe what you need done
2. **Set Budget** - Define your spending range
3. **Wait for Proposals** - Contractors submit their bids
4. **Review & Compare** - Evaluate contractor proposals
5. **Select Contractor** - Choose the best fit for your project
6. **Manage Project** - Track progress and communicate
7. **Complete & Review** - Finish project and rate contractor

#### **For Contractors:**
1. **Browse Projects** - Find projects that match your skills
2. **Submit Proposal** - Create detailed bid with pricing
3. **Communicate** - Answer homeowner questions
4. **Get Selected** - Win the project contract
5. **Complete Work** - Deliver quality results
6. **Get Paid** - Receive payment for completed work
7. **Build Reputation** - Earn reviews and ratings
